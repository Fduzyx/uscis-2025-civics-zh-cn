# USCIS 2025 Civics Test — Unofficial Simplified Chinese Learning Aid

This repository is **not affiliated with or endorsed by USCIS, DHS, or any U.S. government agency**.

The repository provides a Simplified Chinese reference translation of the **128 civics questions and acceptable answers** from the USCIS 2025 civics study material. It is intended for general learning rather than as an authoritative reference for immigration or legal matters.

- [Read the Chinese Q&A](QUESTIONS_128_zh_CN.md)
- [Official 2025 questions and answers (M-1778)](https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/questions-and-answers/2025-Civics-Test-128-Questions-and-Answers.pdf)
- [Official English study guide (M-1175)](https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/brochures/USCIS-2025-Civics-Test-Study-Guide.pdf)
- [Official study-resource updates](https://www.uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates)

The translation was produced with AI assistance and limited editorial review; it is **not** a fully independent line-by-line human translation. Automated structural checks matched all 128 questions and 411 answer choices between the English original and the Chinese draft. The 20 asterisk-marked questions for USCIS 65/20 special consideration were also checked. These are structural checks, not a guarantee of translation accuracy.

The image-rich reconstructed PDF is not distributed here because its historical photographs, artwork, and government insignia have not been fully cleared for independent redistribution.
